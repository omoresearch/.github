## What we are exploring

Most AI still lives inside a chat window. Omo Research explores what happens when agents can **see, move, speak, listen, and act** in a shared environment.

We care about:

- **Voice** — natural, live interaction instead of typing every thought.
- **Space** — agents that understand position, distance, context, and the world they inhabit.
- **Presence** — helpful systems that feel like participants, not panels floating beside the experience.
- **Open source** — practical experiments that people can run, inspect, and build on.

## Omo World

[Omo World](https://github.com/harrythentrepreneur/omo-world) is our flagship embodied-agent project: an AI mentor that plays Minecraft with you as a real player. It walks, mines, builds, speaks out loud, and teaches English through shared action in the game itself.

It is a research lab for agents that learn to be present — not a chatbot with a game-themed interface.
